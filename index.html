Ensō

Most of the day runs on autopilot. Ensō is five or eleven minutes of coming back.

Sit. When the time is up, a chime, and a tree is planted. Keep sitting, and a forest grows — not as a score, but as evidence that you kept showing up.

How it works

  • Choose five minutes or eleven.
  • Put the phone down. Locking it counts as meditating — the session keeps running.
  • Leave the app and stay away, and the session won't count. That's deliberate: the sitting is the point.
  • A chime ends it, and the tree goes into your forest.

The long game

Meditation doesn't fix your week. Changes come slowly, and by the time they arrive they don't feel like changes at all — just how you live. Ensō is built for that timescale, not for a seven-day challenge.

Sitting is the easy part. The hard part is remembering to, and remembering why you started.

The goal is to leave the app. When you no longer need to be brought back to the present, you won't need Ensō. That may take years. It's still the goal.

Private by default

No accounts, no sync, no server. Your sessions live on your phone and nowhere else. The only thing that leaves is anonymous usage analytics, and you can switch that off in the menu. Details in the privacy policy.
